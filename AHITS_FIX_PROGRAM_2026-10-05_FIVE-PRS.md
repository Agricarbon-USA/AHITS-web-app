Apply the fragments: cron expiry scan (`LIVE_VEHICLE`), low-stock scan (`ACTIVE_HUB`, item `deletedAt null`, not RETIRED), feeds (`OPEN_TASK`), vehicles list `_count` and detail `maintenanceTasks` (`OPEN_TASK`), portal `availableUnits` (`ACTIVE_UNIT`), equipment report: `rows`/`assetCount`/`avgUtilizationPct` over `LIVE_VEHICLE`/`ACTIVE_UNIT` while `totalMaintenanceSpend`, events and downtime are summed over **all** in-window tasks including retired assets (D-b); scheduled-maintenance events dated by `lastCompleted` (the complete route nulls `completedAt` when it rolls a schedule forward) and `maintInWindow` counts only tasks with `lastCompleted`/`completedAt` in window or `isDamageReport` (C-8); project deployment counts via `deployment_projects` through `project-associations.ts` (C-12).
