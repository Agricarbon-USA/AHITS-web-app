## 1 · Install it (2 minutes, once)

1. On your phone, open: **https://ahits-web-app-staging-vdz5yvke2a-uc.a.run.app**
2. **iPhone:** Safari → tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**. **Android:** Chrome → tap the **three-dot menu** (top right) → **Install app**.
3. From now on open it **from the icon**, not the browser.
4. Sign in with your **email + your 6-digit PIN**. First time? Use the link Max sent you to choose your PIN.

## 2 · Every working day: the check (about a minute)

1. Open the app. **Home** shows today's one job: **Start daily check** — the right truck is already picked.
2. Answer the questions. Anything you answer **No** needs a short note (add a photo if it helps).
3. Tap **Submit**. **"You're set"** means you're done.
4. Interrupted half-way? Just reopen the app — it picks up where you left off.

## 3 · Bad signal? Keep going

- The app saves to your phone first. You'll see **"check queued, will sync when online"** — it sends itself when signal comes back. Nothing to redo.
- A bar at the top shows anything still waiting to send. Tap it to see the list.
- **Never delete or reinstall the app to "fix" it.** That throws away anything waiting to send. Text Max instead.

## 4 · The words we use

- **Transfer** — moving a truck or gear to someone else. **Entire Rig** = they take everything. **Selected Gear** = just the items you pick. The other person taps **Accept** on their phone; done.
- **Report a problem** — something's broken. Photo + a note. Choose **Still usable** (it stays in your kit) or **Out of service**.
- **Requests** — need materials or equipment? **Requests → New request.** Your bell rings when it's been handled.
- **Scan** — point the camera at a QR sticker to pull up that truck or item.

## 5 · Updates and signing in

- When you see **"A new version of AHITS is available"**, tap **Reload**. Takes a few seconds; nothing is lost.
- Signed out? Sign in again with your PIN. Anything you submitted while signed out is saved and sends after you're back in.
- **5 wrong PINs locks you out for 15 minutes.** Wait it out, or text Max.

Text **Max · +1 419-944-1939** with a screenshot and one line of what happened. Don't reinstall — see §3.
